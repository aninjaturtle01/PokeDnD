# Pokémon d20 Companion — V0.2.4 Premade Playtest Edition

This build is a pre-seeded version of the V0.2.4 player companion. It contains four ready-to-use Level 4 Trainers so a group can start playtesting immediately.

## Premade characters

### Theo — Level 4 Trainer
- Background: Academy Student
- Starter: Charmander Lv. 6
- Kingler Lv. 6
- Diglett Lv. 7
- Growlithe Lv. 7

### Jordan — Level 4 Technician
- Background: Athlete
- Starter: Squirtle Lv. 5
- Voltorb Lv. 8
- Mankey Lv. 8
- Raichu Lv. 9

### Maya — Level 4 Researcher
- Background: Tinkerer
- Starter: Bulbasaur Lv. 8
- Parasect Lv. 9
- Alakazam Lv. 8
- Nidoran♂ Lv. 6

### Ava — Level 4 Nurse
- Background: Performer
- Starter: Squirtle Lv. 6
- Doduo Lv. 5
- Nidoqueen Lv. 8
- Wartortle Lv. 7

All Pokémon are National/Kanto Pokédex No. 100 or earlier and are between Levels 5 and 10. Each Pokémon starts at full HP with the natural moves unlocked for its level under the companion's existing V0.2.1 move-progression rules.

## Important

This edition uses its own browser save key, so it does not overwrite Trainers created in the normal V0.2.x build. The four premades are created automatically the first time this edition is opened. You can still create additional Trainers normally.

The companion remains a tabletop aid rather than a battle engine: it does not roll dice, resolve attacks, calculate damage rolls, or run turns for players.

## Running locally

### macOS
Double-click `start.command`. If macOS blocks it, right-click it and choose Open.

### Windows
Double-click `start.bat`.

Then open `http://localhost:8080` if your browser does not open automatically.
